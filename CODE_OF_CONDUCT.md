# Code of Conduct

PrazyCron contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific.
- Critique code and decisions rather than people.
- Respect different levels of experience and different technical approaches.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. Report serious conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed in `SECURITY.md` rather than in a public issue.
